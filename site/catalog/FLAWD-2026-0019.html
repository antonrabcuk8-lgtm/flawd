FLAWD-2026-0019

Summarization middleware splits a tool call from its result (pairing break)

History compaction separates a tool call from its result and later turns fail with pairing errors.

Failure mechanism

Middleware truncates nested tool blocks without keeping caller/result linkage.

Reproduction

Run code_execution to compute 2+2, then explain the result.

Mitigation

Compaction/summarization must preserve tool-call/result pairing invariants or refuse to compact mid-pair.
